# PRNIT Blog and Content Strategy (6 Months)

This plan is designed to improve both search visibility and AI citation likelihood.  
Audience: founders and CTOs in Series A to Series C startups.

## Positioning

Praveen's positioning is engineering architect for scaling startups.  
Tone is direct, practical, and observation-first.  
No corporate filler. No abstract advice without execution details.

## Publishing Cadence

- Frequency: 2 posts per month
- Length: 800 to 1500 words
- Structure:
  - First 100 words: direct answer to the problem
  - 4 to 7 short sections with practical examples
  - Internal links to service pages and relevant case study
  - Clear CTA in final section

## Voice and Style (Praveen's voice)

- Short sentences.
- Specific claims over broad claims.
- Explain what breaks, why it breaks, and how to fix it.
- Avoid buzzword-heavy paragraphs.
- Use implementation language: architecture, delivery, reliability, ownership.
