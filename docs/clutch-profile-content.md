# Clutch Profile Content - PRNIT

## Company Description (250+ words)

PRNIT (Prnit India LLP) is a software development and engineering architecture company founded in January 2011 by Praveen Singh Shekhawat. We help scaling startups and growth-stage businesses solve architecture and delivery problems that appear when products move from early traction to sustained growth.

Our core belief is simple: most software slowdowns are architecture problems, not just staffing problems. That is why we combine architecture-first thinking with execution by distributed engineering teams. Clients work with us when they need technical direction and delivery capacity at the same time.

We build AI-powered applications, modern web platforms, mobile products (iOS and Android), and cloud infrastructure on AWS and Google Cloud. Our team also supports QA automation, release reliability, and production performance optimization so teams can ship faster without increasing operational risk.

PRNIT serves founders and CTOs in SaaS, healthtech, logistics, and fintech, with additional project experience across manufacturing, media, retail, mobility, and public services. We are headquartered in Jaipur, India and also operate from Bangkok, Thailand, supporting distributed collaboration across regions.

Our engineering model is designed for long-term maintainability and measurable outcomes. Typical engagements focus on reducing release bottlenecks, improving response times, increasing system stability, and accelerating roadmap execution. We align closely with client business goals and provide transparent communication throughout delivery.

If your product is breaking as it grows, PRNIT helps stabilize architecture, improve delivery speed, and build the right engineering foundation for the next stage of scale.
